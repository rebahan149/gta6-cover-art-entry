Golden Hour

Cover Studio turns your own photos into multi-panel, GTA-style cover art — graded, lettered and exported as a PNG. Everything happens on your machine; photos are never uploaded.

1 · Your photos

Select a photo, then click a panel to place it — or span one photo across every panel for the classic one-scene collage.

2 · Layout & colour grade

3 · Caption & lettering

4 · Crop the selected panel

Click a panel on the cover to select it, drag on the cover to move its photo, scroll over the cover to zoom it.

5 · Export the cover
